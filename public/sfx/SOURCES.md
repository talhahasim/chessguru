# Sound sources

- `move.wav`, `capture.wav`, `check.wav`, `castle.wav`, `mate.wav`: cut from "Chess Pieces" (Pixabay sound effect
  #60890, uploaded by freesound_community), Pixabay Content License (free for commercial use, no attribution
  required). Cleaned with a 120 Hz high-pass, trimmed at the transient, faded out, peak-normalised.
  `castle.wav` = the move hit twice, 100 ms apart. Source hits used: move 12.25 s, capture 1.19 s, check 8.17 s,
  mate 21.30 s.
- `whoosh.wav`, `pop.wav`, `impact.wav`: generated by `scripts/gen-sfx.ts`.
